= The gardening model — the entry point (roles, verbs, one gardening)
:toc: left
:toclevels: 3

[NOTE]
====
*Status:* DESIGN, the model's entry point (2026-07-12). This is the ROOT of the seed design: the
gardening model, independent of any realisation. Everything else DESCENDS from it — the
link:seed-broker-spec.adoc[seed-broker spec] realises the GARDENER role, the
link:host-cellar-realisation-spec.adoc[host Cellar realisation] realises the CELLAR role, the domain
handlers realise the SOILS. The link:seed-gardening-lexicon.adoc[gardening lexicon] is the FR-EN
reference vocabulary this model draws its words from.

*Why this doc exists (2026-07-12):* the model — the keystone reframe and the five roles — used to live
INSIDE the seed-broker spec, so a realisation doc (the gardener's mechanism) doubled as the model's front
door. That is the level-mixing this doc removes: the broker is not a sixth role, it is the *name of the
gardener's realisation*. Asking "what role is proper to the broker?" returns nothing — the broker IS the
gardener, incarnate. So the model needs its own home, above the realisations.
====

== The keystone — crossing worlds is a detail; the client sows a seed

The load-bearing reframe. One is tempted to treat the host↔OSGi seam as *the* subject — classloader
realms, the natures of a crossing object, seams to system-export. From the CLIENT's view all of that is
plumbing. The client does not say "I cross a classloader frontier"; it says *sow this seed*. Whether the
seed is sown by switching into Felix, staying flat, or tomorrow going HTTP-remote is behind the door —
the client has ONE relationship and ONE local signal. This is REST's core promise (the client is blind
to the server's implementation), transposed to an in-process seam; it is why the model is
forward-compatible with a future remote split (same contract, a different transport).

== The five roles

The horticultural register names five roles. They are ROLES of the gardening model; a realisation
INCARNATES each one, but the role is the model's, not the realisation's.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Role | What it is (model-level, realisation-agnostic)

| *COMMISSIONER* (provisioner)
| The real driving actor: it WANTS, it LAUNCHES, and it CONSERVES the reaped harvest at home.

| *GARDENER*
| Transversal know-how: knows how to grow correctly on ALL plots, OWNS none, routes a seed to the plot
  that grows it. The client does not till — it asks the gardener to sow (REST: the client requests, the
  server acts).

| *SOILS* / plots
| The DOMAINS — each grows what it owns, and only that.

| *TOOLS* + channel
| Carries the request in and the harvest back, speaking ONLY gardening, holding no plot's vocabulary.

| *CELLAR*
| The COMMISSIONER's conservation organ, its neutral face turned toward the growers. *Not a sixth actor*,
  and *not a single piece of furniture* — it is a COMPOSITION of furnitures (pot, vase, fridge, shelf,
  seed-drawer cabinet, tool board) federated by the conservation ROLE. It holds crops OR tools, sealed,
  never typed by its contents (§ <<cellar-asks>>).
|===

[IMPORTANT]
====
*The cellar is the commissioner's organ, not a third party, and the gardener never touches it.* Two
confusions the model rules out:

* The cellar is NOT a separate actor beside the commissioner — it is the commissioner's *conservation
  register* (the storage face). "The commissioner conserves the harvest" and "the harvest goes in the
  cellar" are one gesture said twice.
* The cellar is NOT the gardener's. The gardener grows and routes; conservation is the commissioner's
  concern. A grower may STORE into / FETCH from the cellar (it is a tool laid in its hand), but the
  cellar is owned by the commissioner, not by the gardener.
====

[[cellar-asks]]
== The cellar is a composition of furnitures + a role — and it asks the gardener where to file

The cellar is not one store with one flat shelf. It is a COMPOSITION of *furnitures* — each a neutral,
STRUCTURED addressing space of one nature (the *pot* = per-resource output, the *vase* = top-level
export, the *fridge* = a history entry, the *shelf* = an entry's address, the *seed-drawer cabinet* =
drawers/cases, the *tool board* = the registered collaborators) — federated by the conservation ROLE. It
is the role that makes these furnitures A cellar rather than a heap; `store`/`fetch`/`neighbours` are the
role's interface, and BEHIND them the role dispatches to the right furniture.

Two furniture laws, model-level:

* *One does not mix.* Each thing has ITS place — this seed in that drawer, that case; this preserve on
  that shelf. Storage is structured, never a heap.
* *The sachet stays sealed.* A furniture knows the PLACE (the _where_), never the CONTENT (the _what_).
  Structure is not opening.

*Legend:* the ROLE (`store`/`fetch`/`neighbours`) is the single face; the furnitures behind it are the
natures it dispatches to. Each furniture is neutral and structured — it holds sealed sachets at
determined places (shelf, drawer/case), never typed by contents. "One does not mix" is why they are
distinct furnitures; "the sachet stays sealed" is why none of them opens a payload.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Filing is not blind, and it is not the caller's job — the CELLAR asks the gardener "what is this?".*
When a grower says `store(parcel, thing)`, it hands an OPAQUE, possibly composite sachet and does NOT say
where. The cellar itself — holding only the sealed envelope — asks the gardener to decompose it
(`sow(SplitCoordinate(domain), sachet)`), and the gardener answers in NEUTRAL parts
(`{ rootstock → { role → value } }` — drawers and cases, never a domain field name). From that answer the
cellar learns both the NATURE (which furniture: pot / vase / fridge) and the PLACE (which case), and files
each part verbatim. So three parties each know one thing, and none opens the sachet:

* the *caller* knows nothing of storage — it says "file this";
* the *cellar* knows the FURNITURE (how/where to file neutral parts) — but asks the gardener for the _what_;
* the *gardener* (via the domain's split reflector) knows the DECOMPOSITION (which parts, which cases).

*Asymmetry with retrieval.* Storing a composite REQUIRES the "what is this?" round-trip. Fetching BY A
NAMED coordinate does NOT: the named coordinate IS the address — filed there, re-read there. The cellar is
therefore itself a CLIENT of the gardener (for `store`), never the reverse; the gardener only ROUTES a
retrieval to the grower, and the grower draws from the cellar it holds. The one case where retrieval again
becomes a question to the gardener is HATEOAS — holding an opaque sachet and NOT knowing the coordinate,
one asks it what it advertises, then `sow`s at the advertised coordinate (the same one verb).
====

[TIP]
====
*Capability as a service — the cellar is the LEAST-capable realisation, so cleverness lives on the
capable side.* A cellar realisation (a Pulumi backend, a git store) knows nothing of any domain. So the
model does not ask it to interpret. If the domain (OSGi) can already RENDER an interpretation — assemble
a typed view from neutral parts — it OFFERS that as a service; it is up to the cellar to CONSUME it or
not, per what it can do. The neutral store returns EVERYTHING neutral (parts by their case name); the
interpretation capability is a service the domain offers, never a requirement on the store. This is why a
`fetch` stamps no domain coordinate: it hands back neutral cases, and the capable side — which asked for
this parcel — knows what to make of them.
====

*Legend:* the caller says only "file this"; the CELLAR (not the caller) asks the gardener to decompose;
the gardener answers in neutral drawers/cases; the cellar files verbatim. For a SINGLE part (the ledger)

BY A NAMED coordinate there is no round-trip at all (the address is the where).

== The verbs — two registers that share no word

The model keeps two verb registers strictly apart, so the decorrelation is stated as vocabulary (a
shared word would re-couple what the roles separate):

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Register | Verb | Gesture

| gardening (the grower, at the field)
| `sow`
| hand a seed to the gardener to grow at the right plot

| gardening (the grower, at the field)
| `reap` / `harvest`
| the grower's hand-over of what grew — a FIELD gesture, never a cellar operation

| conservation (the cellar)
| `store(Parcel, coordinate, végétal)`
| file a végétal at its coordinate (the case) — a FRIDGE (put/peek/take), not an append-only journal

| conservation (the cellar)
| `fetch(Parcel[, coordinate])`
| go get what is stored (re-read by key) — NOT `harvest`: harvest is the field's hand-over, fetch is the
  cellar's retrieval

| conservation (the cellar)
| `withdraw(Parcel, coordinate)`
| TAKE one case out — consume it: gone until re-stored (the fridge; the staging-ring rotation is the usage)

| conservation (the cellar)
| `neighbours(Parcel)`
| the neighbouring cellars — sibling parcels under the same soil
|===

NOTE: the cellar has TWO interfaces (§ link:seed-broker-spec.adoc[seed-broker spec]): `OpaqueCellar`
(the opaque seam the host implements) and `Cellar` (the TYPED, OSGi-only view, decorated by
`CodecCellar` holding a `SeedCodec`). It is a FRIDGE — current-state mutable (store/fetch/withdraw),
with the backend's revision log as an append-only AUDIT axis only. Transactional: `ScenarioCellar`
posts stores as tags on the run's ReportModel and drains them to the durable backend at the ROOT
run boundary.

`harvest`/`reap` STAY gardening verbs attached to the grower; `store`/`fetch`/`neighbours` are the
cellar's. The two registers share no verb — that is the model's guarantee that the field and the store
do not bleed into each other.

== The végétal is opaque — the cellar never looks inside

What the cellar holds — a `végétal` — is SEALED: addressed by a neutral coordinate (the shelf: a
`Parcel`), never read. The cellar knows only the parcel (where) and the gesture (store / fetch), never
the contents (what). This opacity is the model's, not a realisation's convenience: it is why a single
neutral cellar can hold every domain's harvest without knowing any domain — the shelf is neutral, the
crop is sealed.

== One gardening — one commissioner, one gardener, one cellar

A single gardening has ONE commissioner, ONE gardener, ONE cellar. This is the model's multiplicity
rule, and it is what makes "a single transverse cellar" not an oddity but the norm FOR ONE GARDENING: the
cellar is single because the gardening is single, not because of anything about the soil it conserves
onto. A richer deployment with several gardenings would have several commissioners, several gardeners,
several cellars — the model does not forbid it, it simply is not one gardening's shape.

*Different gardening VERSIONS realise the same role over different media.* "One cellar per gardening" is
about count within one gardening; it does not tie the cellar to one medium across gardenings. The
seed-outcluster gardening conserves over the Pulumi backend; the cluster gardening will conserve over a
git-oriented storage — two versions, two cellar realisations of the ONE role, each single within its own
gardening. So a realisation doc must name WHICH gardening it serves; it must never present itself as
"the cellar" (§ <<descend>>).

WARNING: *the boundary of "one" is the GARDENING, never the soil.* A gardening conserves onto some soil,
but the soil is not what makes the cellar single — the gardening is. Confusing "one cellar" with "one
soil/backend" is the mistake corrected on 2026-07-12: the cellar is single per gardening, and would
stay single across several soils of one gardening.

[[descend]]
== How the realisations descend from this model

Each role is INCARNATED by a realisation; the realisation adds the *how*, never a new role.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Model role | Realisation | Spec

| GARDENER
| the *seed-broker* — the one door `sow(coordinate, seed)`, handlers collected by Declarative Services,
  routing by coordinate, the world-crossing hidden behind the door. (The broker is the gardener's name,
  not a role.)
| link:seed-broker-spec.adoc[seed-broker spec]

| CELLAR
| the commissioner's conservation organ — *one role, MANY realisations, one per gardening version.* The
  seed-outcluster gardening realises it over the *Pulumi backend* (`store`/`fetch`/`neighbours`, laid into the
  framework at boot, opaque by coordinate slug). The cluster gardening will realise the SAME role over a
  *git-oriented storage*. The role (and its opacity, its one-per-gardening rule) is the model's; the
  medium is the realisation's.
| link:host-cellar-realisation-spec.adoc[host Cellar realisation] (the Pulumi realisation, for seed-outcluster)

| SOILS
| the *domain handlers* — each domain contributes a `SeedHandler` per coordinate it grows.
| link:seed-broker-spec.adoc#contribution-and-opacity[seed-broker spec — contribution]

| COMMISSIONER / TOOLS
| the *host* (`pulumi-edge` + `seed-outcluster`) — wants, launches (`pulumi up`), conserves via the cellar;
  carries requests + harvests speaking only gardening.
| link:seed-spec.adoc[seed spec — orchestration]
|===

NOTE: "From the system's point of view, the gardening IS the framework" is a REALISATION statement, not
a model one: it holds because THIS system realises the gardener as a DS singleton in one Felix, so one
gardening = one framework. At the model level the rule is simply *one gardening = one commissioner = one
gardener = one cellar*; the framework identity is the seed-broker realisation of it.
