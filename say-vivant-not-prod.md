The user prefers **"vivant"** (French) or **"live"** for the running/deployed system — NOT
"prod" / "production". Asked explicitly on 2026-06-30 ("utilise live a la place de prod :)" then
"ou vivant en francais :)").

**Why:** their vocabulary; "vivant" fits the project's living-system framing better than the
generic "prod".

**How to apply:** when describing the running system, the deployed boot, or the non-test path, say
"le système vivant" / "le boot vivant" / "live", not "prod". Mirrors the existing
provisioned-system distinction (Claude may run anything that does not mutate the live/vivant system).
